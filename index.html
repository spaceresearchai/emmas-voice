About Emma's Voice & Research

How it works, privacy guarantees, and related scientific papers

🔒 100% Private & On-Device: Camera lip/tongue tracking, local neural AI voice, and parent-recorded videos stay privately on this tablet and are never uploaded.

Related Scientific Research

Drimalla et al. (2021) — Molecular Autism

Computer-vision study showing autistic individuals can imitate modeled facial and mouth movements when explicitly demonstrated, benefiting from slower pacing—inspiring our 0.5× Slow Coach and generous hold windows.

JEMImE & LIFEisGAME (ASD Serious Games)

Webcam-driven games where children with ASD copy on-screen expressive avatars with live visual match gauges and joyful reinforcement.

CAMI-2D / CAMI-2DNet (Kennedy Krieger & NTU)

Demonstrated single-2D-webcam automated motor imitation assessment in autism without specialized depth sensors or markers.

LAOLA & ORAR Oral-Motor Therapy Platforms

Combined MediaPipe Face Mesh (478 lip/jaw/cheek landmarks) with cropped mouth-ROI tongue detection to track tongue and cheek exercises non-invasively.

Clinical Note: Playful lip, tongue, and cheek moves build imitation, body awareness, and joint attention, and work best alongside functional speech/AAC practice and a licensed Speech-Language Pathologist (SLP)—which is why Moves Mode sits right next to Words Mode.
